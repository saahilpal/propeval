# ADR-007: Explicit 3-Layer Voice vs. Text Evaluation Boundary (Pipecat Layer-2 Seam)

**Status:** Accepted | **Date:** 2026-11-19

* **Decision:** Implement **Layer 1 (Text + 8 kHz ASR Perturbation & Barge-In Truncation Simulation)** for the 3-week MVP, while explicitly documenting what text simulation *cannot* validate and designing `TargetAgentAdapter` so **Layer 2 (Recorded 8 kHz Audio Replay via Pipecat `1.16.0`, BSD-2-Clause)** and **Layer 3 (Live SIP/Twilio/Exotel Telephony)** plug in without modifying graders or statistical engines.
* **Requirement:** Satisfies FR-07, FR-08 (realistic Hinglish telephony resilience + pluggable target seam) and NFR-02 (fast <90 s CI feedback at ~$0.02/call).
* **Alternatives:** (1) **Audio-First TTS-to-STT Simulation in MVP** (synthesizing buyer audio via TTS and feeding it to live STT on every PR); (2) **Live PSTN/Twilio Calls in CI**; (3) **Text-Only Simulation with No ASR Perturbation**.
* **How the alternatives work:** Audio-first pipelines synthesize buyer turns with TTS, stream PCM/G.711 audio into STT $\to$ LLM $\to$ TTS, and transcribe output. Live PSTN places real carrier calls. Pure text simulation feeds clean grammar without noise.
* **Selection reasoning:** Lau et al. (ISSTA 2023, cited in [Evalgent, Oct 4, 2026](https://www.evalgent.com/blog/replace-manual-test-calls-voice-agent)) found **21%–34% false-alarm rates** when testing ASR with synthetic TTS audio instead of real human recordings, and full audio-to-audio calls cost 10–30× more and run 5–10× slower in CI. Meanwhile, pure clean-text evals miss Indian real-estate ASR homophones (`"crore"` $\to$ `"core"`). Layer 1 with domain ASR perturbations delivers fast, cheap PR gates for prompt/slot/RERA/DNC logic, while explicitly reserving acoustic barge-in (<300 ms), backchannel suppression (*"haan/achha"*), Indic TTS pronunciation (*"₹1.2 Cr"*), and 8 kHz G.711 8-bit quantization ([arXiv:2606.09335](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.09335v1)) for Layer 2 (Pipecat `1.16.0` + recorded human 8 kHz clips + `soxr_hq` resampling + Smart Turn v3.2) and Layer 3.
* **Rejected alternatives:** Synthetic TTS-to-STT in MVP adds high cost, slow CI, and 21–34% false alarms; live PSTN in PR CI is non-deterministic, slow, and expensive; clean text-only simulation ignores Indian telephony reality.
* **Trade-offs:** We gain <90 s CI runs (~$0.02/call) and honest engineering credibility (zero false claims about validating acoustic prosody in text). We defer acoustic barge-in, prosody/false-empathy, and carrier jitter validation to Phase 4 (Layer 2/3).
* **Failure modes:** Engineers forgetting the Layer-1 boundary and assuming a passing text run guarantees good TTS pronunciation or EoT latency (mitigated by explicit UI/CLI disclaimer banners listing unvalidated acoustic dimensions); 8 kHz resampling bugs in Smart Turn ([Pipecat Issue #3844](https://github.com/pipecat-ai/pipecat/issues/3844)) in Layer 2 (mitigated by mandatory `soxr_hq` resampling, never neural upsamplers).
* **Cost:** Minimal MVP cost (~$0.02/call in Layer 1); Layer 2 Pipecat integration deferred to Phase 4 (2–3 weeks + recorded Indian buyer audio corpus).
* **Revisit conditions:** Prioritize Layer 2 Pipecat audio replay immediately once Huvo grants access to anonymized 8 kHz Twilio/Exotel production recordings or when evaluating STT/TTS vendor swaps rather than prompt edits.
